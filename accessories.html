Electric Tracks

Electric Tracks

Electric curtain tracks bring effortless automation to your window furnishings. Open and close your curtains at the touch of a button, via a smartphone app, or with a voice command through Google Home or Amazon Alexa.

Ideal for large curtain spans, high windows, or simply the luxury of full home automation — electric tracks are whisper-quiet, reliable and elegantly slim.

  • Control via remote, app or voice assistant
  • Compatible with Google Home and Amazon Alexa
  • Whisper-quiet motor mechanism
  • Set automated schedules for open and close
  • Available in straight and curved configurations
  • Ceiling or wall mount options

Curtain Rods

A beautiful curtain rod is the frame that completes your window. Our range of decorative rods suits every interior style — from sleek matte black for contemporary homes to brushed gold for a warmer, more luxurious feel.

Available in a variety of finishes, diameters and lengths, with matching finials and brackets. Ideal for eyelet, pinch pleat and tab-top curtain styles.

  • Wide range of finishes: matte black, brushed nickel, gold, chrome and more
  • Multiple diameter options for different curtain weights
  • Matching finials and wall brackets included
  • Custom cut to your exact window width
  • Suitable for eyelet and pinch pleat curtains

Extendable Track

Extendable curtain tracks are a practical and versatile solution for standard windows. They adjust to fit a range of widths without custom cutting, making them a quick and cost-effective option for straightforward installations.

Smooth-running gliders and a robust track profile ensure your curtains open and close effortlessly every day, with a clean and minimal appearance on the wall or ceiling.

  • Adjustable width — no custom cutting required
  • Smooth-running curtain gliders included
  • Available in white and silver finishes
  • Wall or ceiling mount options
  • Cost-effective for standard window widths
  • Compatible with S-Fold and pinch pleat curtains

Curved Tracks
For Bay & Corner Windows

Curved Tracks

Bay windows, corner windows and unusual window shapes require specially bent tracks that follow the exact angle of your window. Our curved tracks are bent to your precise measurements, allowing curtains to flow seamlessly around corners and curves.

Available in both standard and electric motorised versions, curved tracks let you dress even the most architecturally complex windows with elegance and ease.

  • Custom bent to your exact window angle and dimensions
  • Suitable for bay windows, corner windows and curves
  • Available in manual and electric motorised versions
  • Smooth glider system for effortless curtain movement
  • Compatible with all curtain heading styles
