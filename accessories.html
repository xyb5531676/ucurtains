Electric Tracks

Electric curtain tracks bring effortless automation to your window furnishings. Open and close your curtains at the touch of a button, via a smartphone app, or with a voice command through Google Home or Amazon Alexa.

Whisper-quiet, reliable and elegantly slim — ideal for large curtain spans, high windows, or simply the luxury of full home automation.

  • Compatible with Google Home and Amazon Alexa
  • Whisper-quiet motor mechanism
  • Set automated schedules for open and close
  • Available in straight and curved configurations

Curtain Rods

Extendable Track

Extendable curtain tracks are a practical and versatile solution for standard windows. They adjust to fit a range of widths without custom cutting — a quick and cost-effective option for straightforward installations.

Smooth-running gliders and a robust track profile ensure your curtains open and close effortlessly every day.

  • Adjustable width — no custom cutting required
  • Smooth-running curtain gliders included
  • Available in white and silver finishes
  • Wall or ceiling mount options
  • Cost-effective for standard window widths
  • Compatible with S-Fold and pinch pleat curtains
