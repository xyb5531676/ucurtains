Plantation Shutters

Plantation shutters feature wide louvred blades set within a solid frame that fits precisely into your window opening. You can tilt the blades to control light and airflow, or fold the panels open entirely for an unobstructed view.

Unlike curtains and blinds, plantation shutters become part of your home's architecture — they add a classic, finished look that suits everything from Queenslander homes to modern new builds across Brisbane.

  • Adjustable louvres for precise light and privacy control
  • Panels fold open completely for full ventilation
  • Add lasting value to your Brisbane property
  • Suit every home style from classic to contemporary
  • Custom-made to fit any window shape or size
  • Available in full-height, bi-fold and tier-on-tier configurations

Real Timber Shutters

Genuine hardwood shutters with a warm, natural character that improves with age. Each panel has unique grain patterns for a truly bespoke look.

  • Beautiful natural grain and warmth
  • Can be painted or stained any colour
  • Exceptional durability when maintained
  • Highest premium appearance
Most Popular

Faux Timber Shutters

PVC or composite shutters that look like real timber but are completely waterproof and resistant to warping — ideal for Brisbane's humidity.

  • Waterproof — perfect for bathrooms and kitchens
  • Won't warp, crack or fade in Brisbane's heat
  • Lower cost than real timber
  • Same great look, less maintenance
Modern

Aluminium Shutters

Powder-coated aluminium shutters suit contemporary homes and outdoor or semi-outdoor areas. Extremely durable and weather resistant.

  • Ideal for outdoor and alfresco areas
  • Completely weatherproof
  • Available in any powder-coat colour
  • Suits modern and industrial aesthetics
