PVC Shutters

PVC Shutters feature wide louvred blades set within a solid frame that fits precisely into your window opening. You can tilt the blades to control light and airflow, or fold the panels open entirely for an unobstructed view.

Unlike curtains and blinds, PVC shutters become part of your home's architecture — they add a classic, finished look that suits everything from Queenslander homes to modern new builds.

  • Adjustable louvres for precise light and privacy control
  • Panels fold open completely for full ventilation
  • Add lasting value to your Brisbane property
  • Suit every home style from classic to contemporary
  • Custom-made to fit any window shape or size
  • Available in full-height, bi-fold and tier-on-tier configurations
