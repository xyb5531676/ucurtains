Who We Are

U Curtains was founded on a simple belief — that every home deserves beautiful, well-crafted windows. We're a family-owned Brisbane business with over 10 years of experience helping homeowners transform their spaces with custom window furnishings.

Our Story

Built on Passion & Purpose

U Curtains began over a decade ago, born from a genuine love of beautiful interiors and the belief that window furnishings should be more than just practical — they should be a reflection of who you are and how you want to feel in your home.

As a single mum running this business from the ground up, our founder understands first-hand how the small details of a home create the environment where your family's best moments happen. That personal understanding shapes everything we do.

From our base in Acacia Ridge, we serve homes and offices across Brisbane's south side. Every customer gets the same personalised attention — we visit your home, listen to what you want, bring samples, take precise measurements and handle every detail of the installation.

"I started this business because I believe every home deserves windows that make you feel something. Curtains and blinds aren't just coverings. They shape the light, the mood, the memory of a room."
— Founder, U Curtains · Acacia Ridge, Brisbane
