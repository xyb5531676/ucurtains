Polyester Sheers

Styling Tips

How to Layer Your Sheers

Sheers work beautifully on their own, but the most flexible and elegant window solutions combine sheers with other window furnishings for full light and privacy control throughout the day.

Most Popular

Sheers + Blockout Curtains

The classic combination. Sheers filter daytime light and provide privacy; blockout curtains pull across at night or for sleep. Used on a double track or double rod.

Modern Look

Sheers + Roller Blinds

A sleek, contemporary pairing. The roller blind sits within the window recess for privacy and blockout; the sheer curtain frames the window with softness and style.

Maximum Flexibility

Sheers + Zebra Blinds

Zebra blinds give you precise light control; sheers add a soft, decorative layer. Together they offer complete flexibility from open and bright to full privacy.
