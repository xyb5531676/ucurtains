Transparent Sheer

Privacy Sheer

Enjoy the best of both worlds with our privacy sheers. During the day, they allow natural light to filter through while preventing people outside from seeing in. You can see out, but they can't see in — giving you a bright, open feel with complete peace of mind.

Great for bedrooms, ground-floor windows, and street-facing rooms.

  • Daytime privacy without losing light
  • See-out, not see-in effect
  • Softens harsh sunlight
  • Available in a range of weaves and textures
