How to Measure

Measurement Guide

Accurate measurements are the foundation of a perfect window furnishing. While we always recommend our free in-home measure and quote service for the best results, here's a guide to help you understand the process.

Our recommendation: Let us measure for you. Our free in-home measure and quote takes the guesswork out entirely. Book your free appointment →

01

Measure the Width

For inside mount: measure the exact internal width at three points — top, middle and bottom. Use the smallest measurement. For outside mount: measure the full width you want covered, typically extending 15–20cm beyond the frame on each side.

02

Measure the Height (Drop)

For inside mount: measure from the top of the recess to the sill. For outside mount: measure from where you plan to install the rod or track down to your desired length. For floor-length curtains, subtract 1–2cm for clearance.

03

Note Your Window Type

Is it a standard rectangular window, a bay window, a sliding door, or a shaped window? Different window types require different hardware. Take a photo of each window to share with us during your consultation.

04

Check for Obstructions

Note any handles, locks, vents, architraves or cornices that might affect installation. Check whether walls are plumb and square — older Brisbane homes can have walls that are not perfectly straight.

Important: Always measure each window individually — even windows that look the same size are often slightly different. Never assume two windows have identical measurements.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do custom curtains cost in Brisbane? +
The cost depends on the fabric chosen, the size of your windows and the curtain style. As a guide, custom curtains typically start from around $150–$300 per window including installation. We provide a detailed, obligation-free quote after our in-home consultation.
How long does it take from order to installation? +
Most custom curtains and blinds are ready for installation within 3–5 weeks from order confirmation. We'll give you a specific timeframe at the time of your quote.
Do you offer a free measure and quote? +
Yes — absolutely. Our in-home measure and quote is completely free and without obligation. We visit your home, take precise measurements, show you fabric samples and provide a detailed written quote. There's no pressure and no commitment required.
What areas in Brisbane do you service? +
We are based in Acacia Ridge and primarily serve Brisbane's south side including Sunnybank, Carindale, Eight Mile Plains, Moorooka, Salisbury, Rochedale, Macgregor, Runcorn, Stretton, Kuraby, Wishart, Mount Gravatt and more. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, give us a call.
Can I see fabric samples before ordering? +
Yes. We bring a selection of fabric samples to your in-home consultation so you can see and feel the fabrics in your own home under your own lighting conditions. This is the best way to choose the right fabric and colour for your space.
Do you install what you supply? +
Yes — we handle everything from measurement to installation. All our products are professionally installed by our own team to ensure a perfect result and to back it with our installation warranty.
How do I clean my curtains or blinds? +
Cleaning instructions vary by product and fabric. Most fabric curtains and sheers can be gently hand-washed or dry-cleaned. Roller and zebra blinds can be spot-cleaned with a damp cloth. Plantation shutters can be wiped with a damp cloth. We provide specific care instructions for every product we install.
