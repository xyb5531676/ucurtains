How to Measure

Measurement Guide

Accurate measurements are the foundation of a perfect window furnishing. While we always recommend our free in-home measure and quote service for the best results, here's a guide to help you understand the process or take preliminary measurements yourself.

Our recommendation: Let us measure for you. Our free in-home measure and quote takes the guesswork out of the process entirely — we bring professional tools and years of experience to ensure a perfect fit every time. Book your free appointment →

What You'll Need

A steel tape measure (not a fabric tape), a pencil and paper to record measurements, and a step ladder if your windows are tall.

01

Measure the Width

For inside mount (within the window recess): measure the exact internal width at three points — top, middle and bottom. Use the smallest measurement. For outside mount (wall to wall): measure the full width you want the curtain or blind to cover, typically extending 15–20cm beyond the window frame on each side.

02

Measure the Height (Drop)

For inside mount: measure from the top of the recess to the sill at three points and use the smallest. For outside mount: measure from where you plan to install the rod or track down to your desired length — sill, below sill, or floor. For floor-length curtains, subtract 1–2cm from the floor measurement to allow for clearance.

03

Note Your Window Type

Is it a standard rectangular window, a bay window, a sliding door, or a shaped window? Different window types require different hardware and measurement techniques. Take a photo of each window to share with us during your consultation.

04

Check for Obstructions

Note any handles, locks, vents, architraves or cornices that might affect how the curtain or blind is installed. Check whether walls are plumb and square — older Brisbane homes in particular can have walls that are not perfectly straight.

Important: Always measure each window individually — even windows that look the same size are often slightly different. Never assume two windows have identical measurements.

Curtain Fullness Guide

Curtains require more fabric than the window width to hang beautifully. As a general guide: S-Fold curtains require 2x the window width in fabric; Pinch Pleat curtains require 2–2.5x; Eyelet curtains require 1.5–2x. We factor all of this into our calculations during your consultation.

Our Promise to You

Warranty Policy

U Curtains provides an Extended Warranty to further ensure your confidence in our products. This Extended Warranty outlines the additional benefits you will receive, in addition to your existing rights and remedies under the ACL.

Extended Warranty Coverage

  • Duration: This Extended Warranty covers your curtains for a period of 1–15 years from the date of purchase. The exact duration of coverage may vary depending on the specific purchase and circumstances.
  • Major Failures: In the event of a major failure as defined by the ACL, you are entitled to a replacement or a refund.
  • Reasonably Foreseeable Loss or Damage: If your curtains cause any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage, you are entitled to compensation.
  • Failure to Meet Consumer Guarantees: If your curtains fail to be of acceptable quality, and the failure does not amount to a major failure, we commit to repairing or replacing the goods.
  • Consumer Guarantees: We acknowledge and respect your consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law. These guarantees include that the goods will be of acceptable quality and fit for known purposes. This means that your curtains are expected to be safe, durable, free from faults, aesthetically pleasing, and perform as expected.

Claiming Warranty Service

  • Reach out to our customer service team to initiate your warranty claim.
  • Provide a copy of your original purchase receipt or invoice as proof of purchase.
  • We will assess your claim and determine whether it falls within the scope of our Extended Warranty.
  • Depending on the nature of the issue and your entitlement under this Extended Warranty, we will either repair, replace, or provide compensation as applicable.
  • If your claim is related to consumer guarantees under the ACL, we will assess it in accordance with the law and provide the appropriate remedy.

For further details about the Australian Consumer Law and Consumer Guarantees, please refer to the official Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) website. It is essential to understand your rights and obligations as a consumer under the ACL.
