Zebra Blinds

The most versatile blind available — zebra blinds feature alternating sheer and opaque horizontal stripes, giving you seamless control from full transparency to complete privacy with a simple adjustment.

Perfect for Brisbane's bright days, zebra blinds let you filter glare while maintaining your view. Available in a wide range of colours and fabrics to match any interior.

  • Alternating sheer and opaque stripes for variable light control
  • Smooth, easy-glide roller mechanism
  • Available in light-filtering and blockout fabrics
  • Custom-made to exact window dimensions
  • Wide colour range to suit any décor

Roller Blinds
Clean & Minimal

Roller Blinds

Roller blinds are the ultimate minimalist window solution. A single piece of fabric rolls up neatly when open, disappearing almost completely, and rolls down for instant privacy or blockout.

Available in sunscreen, light-filtering and full blockout fabrics — roller blinds are ideal for any room where clean lines and simplicity are a priority.

  • Sleek, streamlined look that suits modern interiors
  • Available in sunscreen, light-filtering and blockout
  • Easy chain or spring operation
  • Child-safe cord options available
  • Hundreds of fabric choices

Roman Blinds
Classic Elegance

Roman Blinds

Roman blinds combine the softness of fabric curtains with the practicality of a blind. They fold into neat horizontal pleats when raised, creating an elegant, structured look that adds warmth and texture to any room.

Available in a wide range of fabrics — from sheer to blockout — Roman blinds are a sophisticated choice for living rooms, dining rooms and bedrooms.

  • Soft fabric folds into clean horizontal pleats
  • Adds warmth and texture to any interior
  • Available in light-filtering and blockout fabrics
  • Wide range of premium fabric choices
  • Custom-made to your exact window size

Honeycomb Blinds
Energy Efficient

Honeycomb Blinds

Also known as cellular blinds, honeycomb blinds feature a unique hexagonal cell structure that traps air and insulates your windows. They reduce heat in summer and retain warmth in winter — lowering your energy bills year-round.

Available in single-cell, double-cell and triple-cell options for varying levels of insulation. A smart, sustainable choice for Brisbane homes.

  • Cellular structure provides excellent insulation
  • Reduces heating and cooling costs
  • Available in single, double and triple cell
  • Light-filtering and blockout options
  • Clean, modern appearance

Vertical Blinds
For Large Openings

Vertical Blinds

Vertical blinds are the classic solution for large windows, sliding doors and floor-to-ceiling openings. The vertical slats rotate to control light direction and stack neatly to one or both sides when open.

Practical, low-maintenance and available in fabric, PVC and aluminium — vertical blinds are a reliable choice for both residential and commercial spaces.

  • Ideal for sliding doors and large windows
  • Rotate slats to direct light precisely
  • Available in fabric, PVC and aluminium
  • Easy to clean and maintain
  • Stack neatly when fully open

Venetian Blinds
Timeless Classic

Venetian Blinds

Venetian blinds offer precise, adjustable light control through their horizontal slats. Tilt the slats to angle light exactly where you want it, or raise them completely for an unobstructed view.

Available in timber, faux timber and aluminium — Venetian blinds suit every style from classic to contemporary and are easy to clean and maintain.

  • Precise light control with adjustable horizontal slats
  • Available in timber, faux timber and aluminium
  • Suits both classic and contemporary interiors
  • Durable and easy to maintain
  • Custom-made to fit any window
