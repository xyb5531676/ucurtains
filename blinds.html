Zebra Blinds
Day & Night Control

Zebra Blinds

Zebra blinds feature alternating sheer and solid fabric bands. By adjusting the position of the bands you can transition from fully open to fully private in one smooth motion.

Their versatility makes them Brisbane's most popular blind style for living rooms, dining rooms and bedrooms.

  • Alternating sheer and solid bands for full control
  • Smooth transition from open to private
  • Clean, contemporary look
  • Cassette head for a neat finish
  • Wide range of colours and textures

Roller Blinds
Clean & Minimal

Roller Blinds

Roller blinds are the ultimate minimalist window solution — a single piece of fabric that rolls up cleanly above the window. Simple, functional and available in hundreds of fabrics from blockout to sunscreen.

Their clean lines make them a favourite for modern Brisbane homes, offices and anywhere a streamlined look is required.

  • Simple, clean single-fabric mechanism
  • Available in sunscreen, light-filtering and blockout
  • Chain or spring-operated
  • Easy to clean and maintain
  • Wide range of colours and textures
  • Affordable and durable

Roman Blinds
Soft & Architectural

Roman Blinds

Roman blinds fold up in neat horizontal pleats when raised and lie flat when lowered, combining the clean look of a blind with the soft warmth of a fabric window furnishing.

Available in a wide range of fabrics from sheer and light-filtering to full blockout.

  • Soft horizontal pleats when raised
  • Available in sheer, light-filtering and blockout fabrics
  • Custom made to your window dimensions
  • Cord or chain operated
  • Adds warmth and texture to any room

Honeycomb Blinds
Insulating & Elegant

Honeycomb Blinds

Honeycomb blinds feature a unique hexagonal cell structure that traps air, providing excellent insulation from both heat and cold — the most energy-efficient blind option available.

Available in single, double and triple cell configurations, and in a range of opacities from sheer to full blockout.

  • Hexagonal cells trap air for excellent insulation
  • Reduces heat entry in Brisbane's hot climate
  • Available in single, double and triple cell
  • Sheer to full blockout opacity options
  • Cordless option for child safety

Vertical Blinds
Practical & Versatile

Vertical Blinds

Vertical blinds feature individual fabric or PVC slats that hang vertically and can be rotated to control light, or drawn aside to allow full access.

Ideal for large windows, sliding doors and floor-to-ceiling glazing.

  • Individual vertical slats rotate for light control
  • Draw to one or both sides for full opening
  • Ideal for large windows and sliding doors
  • Available in fabric and PVC
  • Easy to maintain and clean

Venetian Blinds
Timeless & Adjustable

Venetian Blinds

Venetian blinds feature horizontal slats that can be tilted to precisely control the angle of light entering the room.

Available in timber, faux timber and aluminium — Venetian blinds bring a warm, classic look to any Brisbane home.

  • Horizontal slats tilt for precise light angle control
  • Raise fully to expose window completely
  • Available in timber, faux timber and aluminium
  • Easy to wipe clean
  • Classic look that suits any era of home
