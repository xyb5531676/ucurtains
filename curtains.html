S-Fold Curtains
Most Popular Style

S-Fold Curtains

S-Fold curtains are the modern favourite — their signature gentle S-shaped wave creates a relaxed, flowing silhouette that looks effortlessly elegant whether open or closed. They hang from a specially designed track that guides the fabric into uniform folds.

Perfect for contemporary Brisbane homes, S-Fold curtains work beautifully in living rooms, bedrooms and open-plan spaces where you want clean lines with a soft, luxurious feel.

  • Smooth, uniform wave pattern from top to bottom
  • Requires ceiling or wall-mounted S-Fold track
  • Available in sheer, light-filtering and blockout fabrics
  • Ideal for floor-to-ceiling installations
  • Works on straight and bay windows

Double Pinch Pleat
Classic & Timeless

Double Pinch Pleat Curtains

Double pinch pleat curtains are a classic that never goes out of style. Two pinched folds of fabric at regular intervals create a structured, tailored look with beautiful depth and fullness. They hang from hooks on a traditional curtain track or rod.

A versatile choice that suits both traditional and contemporary interiors, double pinch pleats add a polished, finished look to any room in your Brisbane home.

  • Two pinched folds create a structured, full appearance
  • Compatible with curtain rods and tracks
  • Available in a wide range of fabrics and linings
  • Timeless style suits any interior
  • Easy to open and close smoothly

Triple Pinch Pleat
Luxurious Fullness

Triple Pinch Pleat Curtains

Triple pinch pleat curtains take the classic pleat style to a new level of luxury. Three folds of fabric pinched together at each heading create exceptional fullness and a rich, dramatic look — perfect for formal spaces where you want to make a statement.

With more fabric per pleat than the double version, triple pinch pleats hang beautifully and create a lush, hotel-quality feel in any room.

  • Three pinched folds for superior fullness and volume
  • Creates a rich, formal and luxurious appearance
  • Ideal for floor-to-ceiling statement curtains
  • Available in premium fabrics and linings
  • Compatible with curtain rods and tracks

Box Pleat
Structured & Tailored

Box Pleat Curtains

Box pleat curtains feature flat, structured pleats that fold evenly inward, creating a crisp, tailored appearance. Unlike pinch pleats, box pleats lay flat against the fabric, giving a more contemporary and architectural feel.

The clean lines of box pleat curtains make them ideal for modern Brisbane homes where structure and precision are part of the design aesthetic.

  • Flat, structured pleats for a crisp, architectural look
  • More contemporary than traditional pinch pleats
  • Available in a wide range of fabrics
  • Works beautifully with heavier, textured materials
  • Custom-made to your exact window dimensions

Eyelet Curtains
Casual & Modern

Eyelet Curtains

Eyelet curtains thread directly onto a decorative rod through large metal rings punched into the heading. This creates a relaxed, casual wave pattern and makes opening and closing effortless. The exposed rod becomes part of the design.

A popular choice for Brisbane homes that want a modern, approachable look without sacrificing style. Easy to install and available in a huge range of fabrics and colours.

  • Metal rings thread directly onto a decorative rod
  • Creates a relaxed, informal wave pattern
  • Easy and smooth to open and close
  • Rod becomes a visible design feature
  • Available in a wide range of fabrics and colours
