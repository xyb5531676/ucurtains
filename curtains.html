S-Fold Curtains

S-Fold curtains — also known as wave or ripple curtains — are the modern standard for contemporary Brisbane homes. The fabric forms a continuous, flowing S-shape when drawn, creating a clean, sculptural look that suits both casual and formal spaces.

Installed on a specially designed track, S-Fold curtains glide effortlessly and hang with perfect consistency every time.

  • Continuous wave heading for a sleek, modern look
  • Requires 2x fabric width for ideal fullness
  • Available in blockout, sheer and mid-weight fabrics
  • Ceiling or wall mount on standard or electric tracks
  • Ideal for floor-to-ceiling windows

Double Pinch Pleat

Double pinch pleat curtains are a timeless choice that suits both traditional and transitional interiors. Two crisp folds of fabric are pinched together at regular intervals, creating a structured, tailored heading with a formal elegance.

This heading style works beautifully in living rooms, dining rooms and formal spaces where a polished, classic look is desired.

  • Two fabric folds pinched together for structure
  • Requires 2–2.5x fabric width for ideal fullness
  • Hangs from rings or hooks on a rod or track
  • Suits heavier, more formal fabric choices
  • Classic look that never dates

Triple Pinch Pleat

Triple pinch pleat curtains offer maximum fullness and an opulent, luxurious look. Three folds of fabric are gathered and pinched together, creating deep, generous pleats that pool beautifully when open and hang with striking presence when closed.

This is the heading of choice for formal dining rooms, master bedrooms and any space where you want to make a strong style statement.

  • Three folds per pleat for maximum fullness and luxury
  • Requires 2.5x fabric width — the fullest curtain style
  • Dramatic visual impact especially in heavier fabrics
  • Works with both rods and tracks
  • Perfect for high ceilings and large windows

Box Pleat

Box pleat curtains feature wide, flat pleats that lie neatly against the fabric, creating a clean, architectural heading with a very tailored appearance. Unlike pinch pleats which gather outward, box pleats fold inward, giving a flat, structured look at the top.

This style suits contemporary and Hamptons-style interiors where clean lines and structure are valued.

  • Wide, flat pleats for a clean, tailored look
  • Structured heading that lies flat at the top
  • Works beautifully with linen and textured fabrics
  • Suits contemporary and Hamptons-style interiors
  • Custom-made to your exact window width

Eyelet Curtains

Eyelet curtains feature large metal rings punched directly through the fabric at the top, through which the rod passes. This creates a casual, relaxed wave that differs from track-hung styles — the fabric folds naturally in soft ripples from ring to ring.

Easy to hang and adjust, eyelet curtains are popular in bedrooms, living areas and family homes where a relaxed, contemporary feel is desired.

  • Large metal rings create a casual, soft wave
  • Thread directly onto any standard rod or pole
  • Easy to open, close and adjust
  • Available in a wide range of fabrics and colours
  • More affordable than pleat styles — great for budget-conscious projects
